questionJevtruth

Closer to the fruit?0.93yes
Can still reach its tail?0.62yes

A fixed rule picks the move: rule out anything Jev thinks hits a wall or the body, then score 2 × closer + 3 × tail + 2 × room.

Summary

When a decision model fits, and when it does not

Decision model

  • one-glance judgments
  • triage, routing, gating
  • many questions per state
  • you need a confidence

Code or an LLM with reasoning

  • search and planning
  • very detailed rules
  • anything multi-step
